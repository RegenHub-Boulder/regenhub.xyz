# Members-only newsletter (T-482)

Run: `/admin/newsletter` as an admin (screenshots use sample data; no mail was sent).

1. **Issue list** — a members issue wears a "Members" tag. *"Issues now know who they're for."*
2. **Audience card** — switch Everyone / Members only; the text says exactly who gets it. *"Members only means hub friends, desk members and paid plans of $30 a month or more."*
3. **Send card** — the audience and recipient count sit beside the Send button. *"Before you click, you see who it reaches and how many."*
4. Save the draft after switching, then Prepare audience. Anyone already queued who isn't in the new audience is dropped.
5. Email: header "For RegenHub members", normal unsubscribe footer, no "read on the web" link.
